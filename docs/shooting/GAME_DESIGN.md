# 翠光航路 — original vertical shooter

480×720 logical playfield, upward player fire and downward enemy advance. The visual identity is a deep green orbital ocean with mint allies, magenta/orange hostile fire and ivory/gold machinery. All ship, enemy, stage and boss names are original.

The campaign crosses five sectors and unlocks a sixth secret sector after the final campaign victory. Each sector introduces formations, a midboss at 65 seconds and a boss at 175 seconds. Learning safe routes, concentrating fire on parts, grazing deliberately and choosing when to spend bombs is the intended rhythm. Small player collision geometry must remain readable beneath the decorative hull. Focus movement supports precision.

Eight ships, twenty primary weapons and fifteen supports provide persistent equipment goals. Three ships, three weapons and one support are available immediately. All additional equipment is purchased with currency earned through completed run results; no payment system is provided. Four workshop upgrades have five levels each. Every owned weapon and support also has its own persistent development level 0–5. The campaign theme sequence is city, factory, sky fortress, space, central core and hidden route. Campaign growth and fixed-equipment score competition serve different goals.

Campaign, score attack, daily challenge, boss rush and timed survival labels are reserved by persistence. Actual selectable modes must match the shipped UI/core implementation. Score attack and daily use normalized equipment in core. Rankings are browser-local records, never simulated online players. Daily seed equality does not constitute an authenticated global competition.

The first-play help must explain movement, focus, automatic fire, bomb, special and overdrive. Keyboard and relative touch dragging share simulation input. Visibility loss pauses play. Audio begins after a user gesture; BGM and effects have independent volume. Reduced effects and quality controls accommodate different devices.

Completion is an in-game campaign outcome, not a claim of testing on every physical phone. Touch emulation is useful but cannot certify thermal behavior, mobile audio routing, real refresh timing or hardware accessibility.

Boss arrival is now 175 seconds (midboss 65) to target roughly three to five minutes per stage, including the fight. Root seeded-bot observations before this timing change were 4/4 clears on easy, normal and hard and 1/4 on extreme; this small development sample is not a player success-rate estimate.
