# Weapons and ships

Catalog IDs 0–19 are permanent: shot, rapid, spread, focus, laser, charge, homing, cluster, plasma, chain, explosive, reflect, drill, wave, saber, absorb, turret, drone, burst, fusion. The first three establish straight accuracy, repeated chip damage and wide coverage. Later purchases unlock mechanical alternatives rather than simply replacing every earlier weapon.

Each catalog record supplies damage, shot interval, projectile speed, energy, pierce and range. These are simulation inputs rather than cosmetic labels. Short-range saber trades safety for concentration; slow plasma trades travel time for damage; homing trades target choice for coverage; drill and laser reward aligned enemy columns. Multi-projectile weapons must be balanced against aggregate damage, not individual bullet damage alone. Charge and burst timings need consistent visual/audio cues.

Eight hulls vary movement, precision movement, health, collision radius and damage scalar. IDs 0–7 specials are shield, dash, burst, spread, laser, missile, heal and absorb respectively. Bomb descriptions name the hull's visual theme; core remains authoritative for actual bomb rules. No content description should promise an unimplemented invulnerability duration or guaranteed heal.

Supports 0–14 cover follow, turret, homing, laser, shield, heal, intercept, collect, bomb, reflect, energy, score, rapid, fusion and secret. Their specific effect lives in core; inventory descriptions are concise purpose labels. All numeric changes should be exercised in real combat and costed using run yield, survival and total hit count.

Weapon 5 fires a strong straight charged shot with a 1.1-second interval; weapon 18 fires in sixteen directions. EVOLUTIONS is executable shared data: 0→1 at level4+rapid; 4/5→9 with laserBoost+core; 6→7 at level5+width; 8→13 at level5+rapid; any→19 at level6+three options+core. Temporary pickup power and persistent equipment development are separate systems.

Individual development costs 400/800/1,600/2,800/4,400 credits, common material 3/6/9/12/15, plus two uncommon from the second step, one rare from the third and one epic on the fifth. Only owned equipment can be developed; level five is final.
