# Boss design

Six sector bosses are 軌道殻ミドリ, 硝子鰭ハク, 炉心城クロガネ, 雲龍シデン, 暁核アカツキ and 零星ムメイ. Midbosses introduce each sector's motif at 65 seconds before the main encounter at 175. The names, palette and catalog offsets identify encounters; phase execution is in core.

A boss should combine a readable central body, destructible peripheral parts and changing fire phases. Part destruction contributes actual part statistics and an immediate visual/audio event. Phase transitions require a breathing interval or warning. Rotation, radial patterns and crossing aimed fire create escalating pressure; the final phase should test earlier skills rather than introduce an invisible unavoidable attack.

Health and parts must use maximum-health denominators for truthful bars. A destroyed part stops contributing attacks. Defeating a boss must clear active hazards safely and produce a single stage-clear event. The final sector completion unlocks the secret route through the result recorder. Secret difficulty is optional and should not prevent the standard ending. Exact physical-device performance and full difficulty no-hit certification remain validation tasks, not claims made by this design.

The hidden boss supplies legendary material for the fusion weapon purchase. Secret ownership still requires credits and the campaign-clear flag. Main boss arrival is175 seconds in every sector; part behavior, boss timeout and result handling remain simulation rules.
