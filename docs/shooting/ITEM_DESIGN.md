# Items and supply

Fifteen stable item identifiers are P, S, W, L, M, O, B, H, E, G, ★, R, X, C and ?. They represent power, rapid fire, width, laser support, missile support, options, bomb, repair, energy, overdrive, star, rare material, evolution core, currency/material and unknown supply. Relative drop weights are in data.js. Weight is not a guaranteed per-enemy drop probability: core chooses when drops occur.

Power, currency and energy dominate ordinary supply. Repairs and bombs remain less common so evasion and resource timing retain value. Stars and unknown supply are deliberately rare. Combat evolution and pickup enhancements never purchase permanent hangar ownership. Repair never raises health beyond maximum, bombs and options need caps in core, and resource pickups must respect their corresponding meters.

Collection events are the source of item counters. Do not infer lifetime pickup count from score or elapsed time. Item codex sightings should be populated from actual events. Save materials are five explicit grades; the workshop currently spends common material and credits, while individual equipment development spends uncommon, rare and epic grades. Secret ship, support and weapon unlocks require one rare, epic and legendary material respectively.
