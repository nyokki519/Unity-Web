# 翠星航路 — hard-surface art and event effects pass

The live 390 × 844 playfield was inspected and recorded before editing. Its repeated swept-wing silhouettes made enemy roles look alike; friendly attacks were nearly identical luminous pills; a boss retained pristine armor at low health; explosions were a single radial particle burst; scenery moved as one flat tile. The art direction is an emerald orbital defense force against warm reactor-powered machinery, with a dark central flight corridor and luminous hostile projectiles.

## Implemented

- Eight original player silhouettes (interceptor, needle, heavy twin-pod, scout, armored gunship, swept attack craft, cruiser and dragon), six enemy silhouette families with class colors, and six capital-ship variants. Procedural CanvasTexture artwork includes recessed intakes, panel bevels, louvers, copper service lines, serials, engine chambers and running lights. No third-party art or new dependencies.
- Six original generated painted WebP environments now sit on an independent slow depth plane: emerald canal city, industrial furnace factory, airborne fortress, orbital battlefield, solar core and secret violet sanctum. Detailed architectural scenery remains visible without a black full-screen mask. Sparse foreground infrastructure and a separate star/dust layer add depth. Async loading preserves the procedural fallback; the fallback themes also match the stage catalog.
- GPU projectile shapes distinguish needles, piercing laser ribbons, missile bodies, plasma cores, wave crescents, ricochets and fusion fire; actual shot events create bounded muzzle glows. Chain events retain endpoint-linked electricity.
- Real explosion/part events produce tumbling armor, a short hot spark stage, slowly expanding smoke and fading rings. Boss explosions trigger nine spatially staggered secondary detonations and a final afterglow. The victory report body reveals over 1.35 seconds while Next/Retry/Exit remain immediately visible, clickable and keyboard-focusable. The already-completed simulation and reward recording retain their original behavior. Presentation timelines clear at launch, menu navigation and stage transitions so delayed detonations cannot leak into another scene.
- Boss health darkens armor and exposes warm fires/scorched sections at the real 65% and 28% phase thresholds. Phase transitions have a ring cue and the boss HUD labels the current phase. Destroyed parts still disappear according to actual part state.
- Bomb events have a 180 ms visual contraction, expanding impact, debris and long afterglow. Damage and bullet clearance still happen at the original simulation tick. Item events converge sparks toward the actual pickup/player location.
- Procedural Web Audio adds falling laser chirps, missile noise, low explosion bodies, staggered boss blasts, bomb charge/impact, pickup arpeggios and impact foley. Per-event throttles bound repeated voices; SE/BGM controls and mute remain effective.

## Readability and budgets

Hostile bullets render above scenery, ships, muzzle flashes, debris, particles, rings and the limited screen flash. Their collision radii, trajectories and colors are unchanged. Decorative effects never enter collision or random simulation state. Caps: 1,700 particles, 90 debris fragments, 42 smoke puffs, 12 staged sequences, 48 chain traces and 12 simultaneous muzzle glows. Existing **演出を控えめに** disables shake/flash/parallax animation, removes smoke and reduces particle/debris emission; selecting it also clears existing smoke and trims active effects. Existing **軽量** lowers resolution and emission. Separate persisted controls now allow **画面の揺れ**, **画面フラッシュ**, and **火花・煙・破片の量** (standard / half / none). Turning particles off clears and suppresses spark, smoke, armor-fragment and muzzle pools; enemy bullets, weapon-chain paths, persistent ship damage artwork and range/shockwave rings remain visible. Reduced effects override shake/flash preferences and further reduce emission. Older saves receive the original visual defaults; the profile schema version, save key, replay game version, progression and reward logic are unchanged. The victory report appears immediately with reduced effects; the OS reduced-motion media query also disables its reveal animation.

- Matched 120-frame high-quality Chromium/SwiftShader sample (`comparison-performance.json`): before median **49.9 ms**, p95 **66.7 ms**, 40 draw calls / 312 triangles; after with painted art median **49.9 ms**, p95 **66.8 ms**, 78 draw calls / 412 triangles. Counts are sampled from moving combat and differ with the exact frame. These software-bound samples do not establish equal hardware-GPU cost: the painted pass adds 38 draw calls in this sample, and hardware testing remains necessary. Separate video-recording test measurements vary with software rendering and concurrent host work; they are recorded honestly in `art-report.json`.

This is Linux headless Chromium using software WebGL at 390 × 844. It is not a physical iPhone or hardware-GPU performance claim. On-device thermal, touch feel and audio mixing remain to be checked on actual devices. Audio is synthesized original foley, not a recorded orchestral/sound-library production. Core, combat, data, balance 1.3 and pool modules were not changed. Save-module edits only add defensive defaults/validation for the three presentation preferences; progression, reward and replay logic are unchanged.

Reproduce with system Chromium and Playwright. For video, set `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH` to a writable Playwright installation containing ffmpeg, then run `UNITY_URL=http://127.0.0.1:3483 python test/shooting-art_browser.py`.
